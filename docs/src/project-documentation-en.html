1. About This Document

This document explains the ReLoop web application that has been built from the hackathon proposal, and the technology it uses. It is written for judges, teammates and partners who want to understand what the platform does today.

Reference document: Hackathon Project Proposal — Technology for Environmental Sustainability & Disaster Resilience (file: source/environmental_sustainability_hackathon_proposal.pdf). Section numbers such as “Proposal §3.1” in this document point to that proposal. The proposal explains why the platform is needed; this document explains what has been built and how it works.

Scope: product explanation, features, pages and technology stack. Data storage design is outside the scope of this document.

2. Project Overview

ReLoop is a web-based environmental sustainability platform that reduces waste pollution and encourages the reuse and recycling of waste materials in Myanmar (Proposal §1). It connects individuals, junk shops, organisations and businesses so that unwanted materials reach people who can use them.

The platform answers the three problems in Proposal §2 with three connected components (Proposal §3):

Problem (Proposal §2)ComponentHow the app solves it
No connection between waste generators and recyclersWaste MarketplaceMembers list materials for sale, for free, or as wanted; others send requests; contacts are exchanged after acceptance.
Limited information about waste locationsWaste MapAnyone can pin dumped waste on an interactive map; admins move each report from reported to resolved.
Low awareness about reusing wasteReuse & Recycling IdeasStep-by-step guides per material, with difficulty and safety notes, linked to matching listings.

Who uses it (Proposal §4)

User typeWhat they can do in the app
IndividualSell, give away or request materials; report waste locations; follow their reports.
Junk ShopPost WANTED listings for scrap, buy bulk recyclables, sell sorted material.
OrganisationCollect materials for projects, run clean-ups, report and follow waste sites.
BusinessSource raw material for recycling or production; sell production offcuts.
AdministratorVerify reports, review listings, manage members, categories and ideas.

3. Features Delivered

3.1 Waste Marketplace (Proposal §3.1)

Members browse listings as cards and can search by keyword and filter by listing type, material category, city and status, and sort by newest, price or quantity. Each listing shows the material, quantity and unit, price where applicable, location (city, township and optional map pin) and its status.

Listing typeMeaning in the app
For SaleThe owner sells material at a price per unit (in MMK). A price is required.
FreeThe owner gives material away. No price is stored.
WantedThe owner is looking for material. Other members offer it to them.

Listing owners can mark a listing Active, Paused, Reserved, Completed or Closed at any time.

3.2 Request, Exchange and Contact Privacy (Proposal §3.1)

  1. A member creates a listing.
  2. Another member opens it and sends a request with a quantity and a message. On a Wanted listing this is an offer.
  3. The listing owner accepts or rejects the request. Only the owner can do this, and members cannot request their own listings.
  4. After acceptance, both sides see each other’s Telegram, phone or email and arrange the exchange themselves.
  5. The owner marks the exchange as completed. The requester can cancel a pending or accepted request.
Contact privacy: contact details never appear on listings or public profiles. They are shown only to the listing owner and the requester, and only once a request is accepted or completed. The MVP does not process payments or manage delivery, as stated in the proposal.

3.3 Waste Map (Proposal §3.2)

Reports appear as coloured pins on an interactive OpenStreetMap map, with a side list that can be searched and filtered by status and waste category. Clicking a pin opens the report: category, description, estimated amount, location, reporter and a progress timeline. New reports are created by clicking the map or using the device location.

StatusMap colourMeaning
REPORTEDRedSubmitted by a community member, awaiting verification.
VERIFIEDAmberChecked by an administrator and queued for collection.
COLLECTEDBlueThe waste has been picked up.
RESOLVEDGreenThe site is clean and the case is closed.

As in the proposal, a waste report is separate from a marketplace listing: reporting a site does not put the material up for sale.

3.4 Reuse & Recycling Ideas (Proposal §3.3)

Ideas are prepared by administrators (not AI-generated) and can be filtered by material and by method (Reuse or Recycle). Each idea has a description, numbered steps, difficulty, safety notes and links to matching listings. The demo includes 14 ideas, covering every example in the proposal:

MaterialIdeas in the app
Plastic bottlesPlant pots, eco-bricks, selling sorted PET
CardboardCompost “browns”, selling to box makers
Glass bottlesDecoration, reusable storage jars
Old tyresGarden planters
Scrap metalSelling aluminium cans to scrap buyers
Old clothesCleaning cloths, rag rugs
OtherSafe e-waste drop-off, balcony compost, pallet bench

3.5 Supporting Features (Proposal §6)

  • Registration and login — sign-up with account type and private contact details; login by email.
  • Profiles — public profile with bio, location, listings, reports and activity counts.
  • Dashboard — My listings, incoming requests, sent requests and my waste reports in one place.
  • Administrator console — update report status, review listing status, deactivate or reactivate members, enable or disable categories, publish or hide ideas.
  • Homepage and About page — platform statistics, latest listings and reports, and the project’s purpose and vision.
  • Responsive interface — works on phones, tablets and desktops, with light and dark themes.
  • English and Burmese — the whole interface switches language from the header, including dates and Myanmar numerals.

7. Running the Project

Requirements: Node.js 20 or later and npm. From the project folder:

cd web
npm install
npm run dev        # then open http://localhost:3000

There is no password for the demo. Use the account menu (top right) to switch between demo users, or log in with a demo email on the login page. The language button in the header switches between English and မြန်မာ.

Demo userRoleGood for trying
Aung Min ThuIndividualAccepting incoming requests; seeing contacts after acceptance
Shwe Pyi Junk ShopJunk shopWanted listings; a pending request and an accepted one
Green Yangon InitiativeOrganisationWaste reports and eco-brick listing
Platform AdminAdministratorAdmin console: report status, members, categories, ideas

Changes made in the demo (new listings, requests, reports, accounts) are kept until the server restarts.

8. Next Steps

  1. Connect the planned PostgreSQL database with Prisma (Proposal §9) in place of the demo data; the pages do not need to change.
  2. Replace demo login with secure session or JWT authentication and password checks.
  3. Add photo upload for listings and waste reports using Cloudinary or Supabase Storage.
  4. Deploy to Vercel and run user testing with junk shops and community groups in Yangon and Mandalay.
  5. Review the Burmese wording with native speakers before launch.

9. Conclusion

The MVP delivers the three components set out in the proposal — a waste marketplace with private contact exchange, a community waste map and reuse and recycling ideas — together with profiles, a dashboard, an administrator console and a fully bilingual interface. It is ready to demonstrate how the platform addresses environmental waste problems in Myanmar.
